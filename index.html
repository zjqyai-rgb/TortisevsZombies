Turtles vs Zombies

Defend the turtle nesting beach and the coral reef nursery from the zombie tide!

How to play

  • Click floating pearls to collect them. Pearls pay for turtles.
  • Pick a turtle card (or press 1–8), then click a tile to place it.
  • Zombies march in from the sea. Stop them before they reach the nest on the left!
  • Each lane has one crab as a last line of defence. It sweeps the lane once.
  • S shovel · P/Space pause · Esc cancel · right-click cancel
